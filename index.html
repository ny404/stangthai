BRIDGING THE GAP

Located in the heart of Windsor Terrace, Stang Thai delivers more than just a single culinary experience. Born from our deep Thai roots and an absolute love for Vietnamese street food, we have created a space where two vibrant cultures are prepped harmoniously.

From the spicy, exotic wok-fire of our Pad Kra Pow to the comforting, complex broth of our slow-simmered Pho, every dish is a celebration of Southeast Asian heritage.
